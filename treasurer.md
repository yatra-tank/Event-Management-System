Treasurer Module – Event Management System

Overview

The Treasurer module is responsible for managing the financial activities related to events. It helps keep track of event budgets, expenses, payments, and overall financial records.

Responsibilities

- Manage the budget for each event.
- Record and track event-related expenses.
- Maintain payment and transaction records.
- Monitor the available budget and remaining funds.
- Keep financial information organized and up to date.
- Generate financial summaries for events.
- Help ensure that expenses stay within the approved budget.

Main Features

1. Budget Management

The treasurer can create and manage budgets for individual events.

2. Expense Tracking

All event expenses can be recorded with relevant details such as:

- Expense name
- Amount
- Date
- Category
- Description

3. Payment Management

The module can maintain records of payments made for venues, equipment, food, decorations, transportation, and other event requirements.

4. Financial Summary

The treasurer can view the total budget, total expenses, and remaining amount for an event.
